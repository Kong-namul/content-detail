Overview

Fieldnote sells technical outdoor gear to people who plan trips months ahead and shop on a phone, often from somewhere with one bar of signal. Its old store treated a tent and a stove as unrelated products, and every country ran a slightly different copy of the site.

Ondo Studio was asked to turn that patchwork into one platform. We started in the field, walking trails with guides and customers, and discovered that people never shop for a single item. They shop for a trip, and the store should think the same way.

The result is a headless storefront built around kits, backed by a shared design system and a content model that translates cleanly. It launched in eighteen markets on the same day, and the team has shipped weekly ever since.

01

Shop the way you pack

Kit Builder

Instead of browsing a catalogue, shoppers start from the trip. Pick a route, a season and a pack size, and the builder assembles a kit item by item, flagging weight, overlap and anything that will not fit the bag. Every swap updates the total in real time, so the plan stays honest.

APP SCREENS · 3:2

Trail-tested Filters

Product data was rebuilt around how gear is actually used: temperature rating, packed volume, repairability, terrain. Customers filter by the conditions they expect, not by the jargon in a spec sheet, and the results page explains why each item made the list.

INTERACTIVE · 3:2

Field Notes Journal

Staff, guides and customers file short reports from the trail, each tied to the products they carried. The journal feeds product pages with real wear stories and gives the editorial team a steady stream of material to build campaigns from.

PHOTO · 3:2

02

Fast on a mountain signal

Edge-rendered Pages

Pages are assembled at the network edge, close to wherever the shopper happens to be standing. Critical content arrives first and personalisation follows, so a product page is readable on a weak connection before the carousel has even started loading.

DEVICE MOCKUP · 3:2

A Lighter Image Pipeline

Every photograph now passes through a pipeline that picks format, crop and weight for the device in hand. Heavy hero images shrank by more than half with no visible loss, and art direction survives the trip from studio to small screen.

LOOP VIDEO · 3:2

03

One system, eighteen markets

Design Tokens

Colour, type, spacing and motion live in a single token set that every market inherits. A regional team can adjust a headline size or a promo colour without forking a component, which keeps eighteen storefronts recognisably one brand.

IDENTITY · 3:2

Localization at Launch

Content, measurements, currencies and legal copy were modelled as data from day one. Translators work inside the same editor as merchandisers, so a new market launches with its own voice instead of a pasted-over English template.

APP SCREENS · 3:2

After Launch

Ondo stayed on to coach the in-house team through the first ninety days: shared rituals, a contribution guide and a quarterly audit. New markets now ship in weeks, and the library keeps growing without a central bottleneck.
