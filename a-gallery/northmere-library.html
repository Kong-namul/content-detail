About

After four years closed for restoration, Northmere Library reopened its doors to a city that had changed around it. It needed an identity that could greet a toddler and a pensioner with the same warmth.

We built a flexible mark from the library’s own architecture, a typographic voice that works from a bus stop to a bookspine, and a signage system that turns a Victorian floor plan into something anyone can read at a glance. Everything is designed to be printed in-house and repaired by the staff.
