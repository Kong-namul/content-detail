Tamo Bank

Money, made lighter

Banking · 2026

Tamo began as a phone-first account for students: round, friendly, a little cheeky. Within five years it had a million young customers who treated it like a group chat that happened to hold their money.

Then those customers grew up, and their parents started asking for a login. Tamo needed a brand big enough for a first paycheque and a first mortgage, without losing the lightness that people loved. We rebuilt it from the logo to the launch.

Challenge

One bank, two generations

Research showed two Tamos living side by side. Twenty-somethings loved the playfulness but wanted to be taken seriously. Customers over forty trusted the product but found the tone a bit childish. Our brief was to stop choosing between warmth and credibility, and make one voice that carries both.

Graphic System

A circle that holds everything

The old logo was a bubble. The new mark is a circle that opens and closes like a coin purse, and it became the container for everything else: avatars, buttons, charts, card art. One shape, scaled up and down, keeps the whole system calm and recognisable at any size.

Typography

Numbers you can trust

A bank is mostly numbers, so we started there. We chose a geometric grotesque with open, even digits and tuned the spacing so balances line up in a column at a glance. Headlines stay big and friendly, while the small print stays plain and precise.

Illustration

Room for small joys

Money apps are full of waiting: for a transfer, for payday, for a goal. We filled those pauses with small hand-drawn scenes built from the same circle: a coin that naps, a jar that fills with light. They give the product a smile without getting in the way.

Digital Design

From sign-up to first savings

We redesigned the journey around a single question: how fast can a new customer save their first euro? Sign-up takes three taps, the home screen shows one clear balance, and a savings goal can be started from any screen. Every step uses the circle as its anchor.

Launch

Lighting up the city

The campaign ran for six weeks across the city, with one idea: the circle turns into whatever you are saving for. Tram wraps, station takeovers and a glowing installation in the main square let people see the new bank before they ever opened the app.

Outcome

Lighter, for everyone

Six months after launch the numbers told the same story as the streets. New accounts climbed past two million, the app held a rating close to five stars, and the customers we had worried about, those over forty, became more than a third of everyone who joined.
